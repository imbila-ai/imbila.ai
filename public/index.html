03 · Our signature IP

The 4D AI-Fluency Framework

Working with AI well comes down to four competencies. They’re the backbone of everything we teach and advise on — based on the framework by Prof. Joseph Feller & Prof. Rick Dakan, in partnership with Anthropic.

De

Delegation

Deciding what to give AI, what to keep, and what to do together.

De

Description

Communicating with AI to get high-quality results.

Di

Discernment

Critically evaluating AI output — accurate vs plausible-but-wrong.

Di

Diligence

Using AI ethically, accurately, and responsibly.
