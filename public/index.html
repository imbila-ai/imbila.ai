About

Who we are

The company

Imbila.AI is a South African AI research company. We develop methodology, architecture, and education for AI agent systems. Our work powers the 2nth.ai platform and its partner ecosystem.

Founded by Craig Leppan. Based in South Africa. Building for the world.

The name

Imbila is a Zulu word meaning "rock hyrax" — a small, unassuming animal found across Southern Africa. Despite its size, it's the closest living relative of the elephant. Intelligence doesn't need to be large to be powerful.

That's our philosophy: small, focused AI capabilities that compose into something much larger than the sum of their parts.
